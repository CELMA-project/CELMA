Update 2026-11:
===============
- properZModified and simpleX: the broken convergence came from the test
  functions, not the operator. Neither continued smoothly through the axis,
  so the axis boundary condition, f(-rho, theta) = f(rho, theta + pi), set
  wrong ghost cells. With f even in rho (simpleX) and going as rho^2 with an
  even envelope (properZModified) both converge at order 2.
